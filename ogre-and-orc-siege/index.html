How to play

Build

Pick a tower from the shop and click a tile inside the lit build lane that follows the route, then press Confirm build — the gold is only spent when you confirm, and tapping another tile moves the ghost first. The lane is five tiles wide; everything outside it is scenery you cannot build on. Towers cannot stand on the dirt path itself, on each other, or on the red warded ground around the spawn portal. Click a placed tower to upgrade it or sell it for 70% of what you invested.

Defend

Monsters march from the western portal to your gate in the east. Each stage lays out a fresh route that holds for all ten of its waves, splitting into up to three lanes from stage two. Study the glowing path, then press ▶ in the top right to start the wave.

Upgrade

Every tower has ten levels, and each upgrade costs more than the last. Upgrades raise damage and fire rate — never range, so coverage comes from where you build. Towers come in five technological eras: each prestige opens the next one, from timber archers up to gravity wells. Locked towers stay on the shelf so you can see what is coming.

Survive

The campaign runs fifty waves in five stages, and a boss closes each one — the Siege Titan leads the last stand at 5-10. Every leak damages the gate. If the gate falls, the kingdom falls with it. Each prestige adds another fifty waves before the next one is offered, up to a 500-wave campaign at 50-10, and both monsters and bosses grow tougher the deeper the run goes.

From prestige 3 the battlefield itself opens up to 45×45 and the horde gains a second portal in the west, so assaults arrive down two separate lanes. Towers held behind prestige are hidden from the shop until you earn the level.

From 2-2 the horde sends Infiltrators: hooded saboteurs with twice the health of an ordinary monster and a stubborn streak against slow and freeze. Five seconds after it arrives, an Infiltrator detonates a pulse that shuts down every tower on the map for three seconds, then again every fifteen seconds until it dies or reaches the gate. Tesla bolts that strike one directly hit for double damage — sniping them early is worth it.

Beat 5-10 and you can Prestige: the campaign restarts at 1-1 with every enemy 20% tougher, while your upgraded towers wait in storage.

Endless has no last wave, and it is fought on its own 50×50 field. The portals and their lanes are fixed for the whole run and your towers stay exactly where you put them; every ten waves another portal opens beside the first, adding a new lane to march down, up to six. The packs keep thickening and enemy health climbs past anything the campaign curve reaches, and the run ends only when the gate falls — the deepest wave you survive is kept as your record.
